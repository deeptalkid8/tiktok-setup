Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tiktok Setup (“Service”) handles information when an authorized user uses the Service to connect a TikTok account and publish content through TikTok for Developers APIs.

1. Information We Process

Depending on how you use the Service, we may process information needed to operate the workflow, such as TikTok authorization information, account identifiers provided by TikTok, video files, captions, publication results, and technical information required to process requests.

2. TikTok Information

When you authorize the Service through TikTok, the Service may receive information and permissions made available through the TikTok APIs that you authorize. The Service uses this information only for the purposes described in the Service, including connecting the authorized TikTok account and performing requested publishing actions.

3. User Content

Video files and captions submitted for publishing are processed to perform the requested workflow. The Service does not claim ownership of your content.

4. Use of Information

Information may be used to authenticate an authorized account, process and publish requested content, return publication results, troubleshoot technical problems, maintain security, and operate the Service.

5. Sharing

Information may be transmitted to third-party services required to provide the requested functionality, including TikTok for Developers APIs. We do not sell personal information.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the requested workflow, maintain security, comply with applicable obligations, or resolve disputes. Where data is no longer needed, it may be deleted or anonymized.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed by the Service. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Revoking Access

You may revoke the Service’s access to your TikTok account through the applicable TikTok account controls. Revoking access prevents future use of the revoked permissions by the Service.

9. Data Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of personal information held by the Service by contacting the Service operator through the contact method provided with the Service. The request should identify the account or information concerned. Information that must be retained for legal, security, or dispute-resolution purposes may be retained as required.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Service or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, please contact the Service operator through the contact method provided with the Service.
