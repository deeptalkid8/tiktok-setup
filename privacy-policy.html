Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how WhatsApp Content Publisher handles information when users submit videos and captions for publication to social media platforms through an automated workflow.

2. Information We Process

The workflow may process the following information:

  • Videos, captions, and other content submitted by users.
  • WhatsApp sender identifiers needed to validate authorized requests.
  • Social media account authorization credentials and access tokens.
  • Publication status, post URLs, and technical error information.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only as needed to:

  • Receive and validate publication requests.
  • Upload user-authorized videos and captions to connected platforms.
  • Return publication status and resulting post links.
  • Troubleshoot workflow errors and maintain service security.

4. Data Sharing

Submitted content and necessary account authorization data may be sent to the social media platforms selected for publication and to service providers required to operate the workflow. Those services process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

We do not sell personal information or use social media data for unauthorized purposes.

5. Data Storage and Security

The workflow may temporarily process content and store execution logs or publication results. Access tokens and other credentials should be stored in protected configuration or credential storage and must not be shared publicly. Reasonable measures are used to restrict access to workflow data.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary to operate, troubleshoot, and maintain the workflow. Users who need content or workflow data deleted may contact the workflow operator through the contact channel provided by the operator.

7. Third-Party Platforms

Publication to X, TikTok, or other supported platforms is subject to the applicable platform rules, permissions, and privacy policies. This project is independently operated and is not affiliated with or endorsed by those platforms.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the workflow or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or requests concerning personal information, contact the workflow operator through the contact channel provided by the operator.
